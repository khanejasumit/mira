Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

Mira is a personal assistant app used only by its owner on his own Android phone. This policy explains how it handles data from his Google account.

What Mira accesses

  • Gmail: reading messages, writing drafts, and sending mail the owner has approved.
  • Google Calendar: reading and adding events.
  • Google Drive: reading files.
  • Google Tasks: reading and adding tasks.

How it is used

Google data is used only to answer the owner's own requests on his phone, for example to read out his morning briefing or draft a reply he asked for. To do that, the relevant part of a message or event may be sent to the AI service the owner has chosen (Anthropic's Claude or Google's Gemini) under his own account with that service.

What Mira never does

  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google data to anyone else.
  • It does not use Google data for advertising, or to train AI models.
  • It does not keep Google data on any server: the sign-in and anything it reads stay on the owner's phone.

Mira's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google in Mira at any time, or remove Mira's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
