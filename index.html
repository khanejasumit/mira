Mira

Mira is a personal voice assistant for Android, built for and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

Mira answers spoken requests on its owner's phone: it reads out the day's calendar and important mail, drafts replies, adds tasks, and works with the services its owner has connected.

Google account access

When its owner signs in with Google, Mira can read his Gmail, write drafts and send mail he has approved, read and add calendar events, read Drive files and read and add Tasks — only to answer his own requests.
